How to read these crops

Blurry, grainy crops come from poor views (forest canopy, rubble). Several sightings of one object help: look for the shape in any of them. If you cannot tell, ask for a closer look.

Positions are the last ones this station heard. "Lease" is the drone's right to its job: it ends at the battery deadline, after which the job is given to another drone.
